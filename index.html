Who We Are

A Print Studio Built By People Who Actually Love Ink

Sketch N Wear started with a simple frustration: too many "custom" t-shirt shops treated every order like a copy-paste job. We wanted a studio where a birthday order gets the same care as a 500-piece corporate rollout.

We work across screen printing, DTF and heat transfer, so we're never forcing your design into the one process we happen to own. Small family orders, festival batches, college fests, sports jerseys, corporate merchandise — each one gets matched to the technique that will actually hold up in the wash.

Quality isn't a slogan. It's checking registration on every screen before a run starts, curing prints at the right temperature, and being honest when a design needs a tweak to print cleanly.

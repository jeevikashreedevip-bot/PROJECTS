Our Journey

From One Press To A Full-Service Print Studio

A studio a customer trusts on the first order and the fiftieth.

Sketch N Wear didn't begin with a business plan — it began with a single manual press, a stack of blank tees, and a belief that custom printing shouldn't feel like a factory line.

Our mission is direct: match every design to the printing method, fabric and finish that will actually make it last, and communicate honestly at every step, from the first proof to the final delivery.

As demand for personalised apparel grows across colleges, businesses and events, we're investing in better colour-matching equipment, wider fabric ranges and faster turnaround — without ever treating quality checks as optional.
